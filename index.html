About Shadai

Shadai International Clearing & Forwarding (Private) Limited is a trusted logistics partner providing comprehensive customs clearance, sea freight, air freight, and inland transportation services across Sri Lanka. We are committed to delivering reliable, efficient, and cost-effective logistics solutions, ensuring every shipment is handled with professionalism, compliance, and attention to detail from origin to destination.

Why Choose Shadai?

At Shadai International, we understand that every shipment is unique. Our experienced team works closely with Sri Lanka Customs, shipping lines, airlines, and transport partners to ensure smooth cargo movement with minimal delays. We provide end-to-end logistics solutions, accurate documentation, timely communication, and personalized customer support, giving our clients complete confidence throughout the shipping process.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision
To become Sri Lanka's most trusted and preferred clearing and forwarding company by delivering innovative, reliable, and customer-focused logistics solutions that connect businesses with global markets.

Mission
To provide seamless customs clearance, freight forwarding, and transportation services through professionalism, integrity, and operational excellence while building long-term relationships based on trust, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.
