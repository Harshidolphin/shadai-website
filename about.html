BUILT AROUND THE PAPERWORK NOBODY ELSE WANTS TO CHASE

Shadai International Clearing and Forwarding (Private) Limited is a Sri Lankan customs brokerage and freight forwarding company providing reliable, efficient, and professional logistics solutions for businesses and individuals. We specialize in customs clearance, sea and air freight forwarding, cargo documentation, import and export coordination, and inland transportation.

From the preparation of essential shipping documents to customs processing, cargo release, and final delivery, we coordinate each stage carefully to help ensure your shipments move smoothly through Sri Lanka's ports and airports.

Our team works closely with Sri Lanka Customs, port authorities, shipping agents, airlines, and other relevant stakeholders to coordinate clearance and cargo-handling requirements efficiently. We understand that every shipment involves important deadlines, documentation, regulations, and costs, which is why we focus on accuracy, clear communication, and timely coordination throughout the process.

Whether you are importing vehicles, containerised cargo, commercial goods, or general shipments, Shadai International provides practical end-to-end support tailored to your requirements.
