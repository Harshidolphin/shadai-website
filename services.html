Services

End-to-end clearing & forwarding.

01 / CUSTOMS

Customs Clearance

Comprehensive customs clearance solutions, including import and export documentation, tariff classification, duty and tax assessment, customs declarations, regulatory compliance, and efficient cargo clearance through Sri Lanka Customs, ensuring fast, accurate, and hassle-free processing.

02 / SEA FREIGHT

Sea Freight Forwarding

We provide reliable sea freight forwarding solutions for containerised, break-bulk, and general cargo moving to and from Sri Lanka. Our team coordinates shipment bookings, shipping documentation, cargo tracking, port procedures, and communication with relevant shipping and logistics partners. From the initial booking to the arrival and clearance of cargo, we help ensure each shipment is handled efficiently and with proper documentation.

03 / AIR FREIGHT

Air Freight Forwarding

For urgent and time-sensitive shipments, our air freight forwarding service provides efficient coordination from origin to destination. We work with established air freight partners to arrange shipment bookings, prepare required documentation, coordinate customs procedures, and monitor cargo movement. By preparing documentation and clearance requirements in advance, we help minimise unnecessary delays when cargo arrives at the airport.

04 / VEHICLES

Vehicle Clearance

We provide professional customs clearance assistance for imported vehicles, supporting both vehicle dealers and individual buyers throughout the clearance process. Our team assists with documentation, customs procedures, duty and tax-related requirements, valuation-related documentation, and coordination with relevant authorities.

05 / TRANSPORT

Inland Transport

Once cargo has been cleared, reliable transportation is essential to complete the logistics process. We arrange inland transportation of cleared cargo from ports, airports, and other designated locations to warehouses, business premises, or final destinations.

06 / ADVISORY

Documentation & Advisory

Accurate documentation is one of the most important parts of successful customs clearance and international trade. Our documentation and advisory service helps importers and exporters understand the requirements that apply to their cargo before it reaches the port or airport.

We provide practical guidance on customs-related documentation, tariff classifications, permits, supporting documents, and regulatory requirements. By identifying documentation requirements early, we help clients reduce the risk of avoidable delays, corrections, and additional costs during the clearance process.
