Services

End-to-end clearing & forwarding.

01 / CUSTOMS

Customs Clearance

Comprehensive customs clearance solutions, including import and export documentation, tariff classification, duty and tax assessment, customs declarations, regulatory compliance, and efficient cargo clearance through Sri Lanka Customs, ensuring fast, accurate, and hassle-free processing.

02 / SEA FREIGHT

Sea Freight Forwarding

Booking, tracking, and coordination of containerised and bulk cargo shipped by sea, inbound and outbound.

03 / AIR FREIGHT

Air Freight Forwarding

Time-sensitive cargo handled through air freight partners, with documentation prepared ahead of arrival.

04 / VEHICLES

Vehicle Clearance

Clearance of imported vehicles for dealers and individual buyers, including tax and duty documentation.

05 / TRANSPORT

Inland Transport

Delivery of cleared cargo from port to warehouse or destination, via our own fleet or trusted contractors.

06 / ADVISORY

Documentation & Advisory

Guidance on tariff codes, permits, and regulatory requirements before cargo ever reaches the port.

How it works

From booking to release.

STEP 1

Send your shipment details

Bill of lading, invoice, and packing list — we review and flag anything missing early.

STEP 2

We prepare & lodge documents

Customs declaration and supporting paperwork filed ahead of vessel or flight arrival.

STEP 3

Clearance & inspection

We coordinate valuation, inspection, and duty payment directly with the authorities.

STEP 4

Delivery

Cleared cargo moved to your warehouse or address by our transport network.
